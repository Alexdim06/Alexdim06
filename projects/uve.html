01 / What it is

One extension.
One product system.

UVE sits on top of any compatible HTML5 player and lets people watch the way they actually work: faster lectures, louder quiet audio, and saved preferences per site.

The extension is the product people install. SystemaWeb is everything around it: the public site, guides, pricing, and a one-time Pro license processed through Stripe. Together they are not two side projects — they are one ecosystem with a clear job for each layer.

02 / What it does

Control the player
the site forgot to give you.

Built for YouTube, Udemy, Coursera, and any page with a standard HTML5 video or audio element.

01

Playback speed

Fine control from 0.1× to 16×, with sliders, presets, and A/D keyboard shortcuts for lectures and tutorials.

02

Volume boost

Web Audio amplification up to 600% so quiet course videos and laptop speakers stay usable.

03

Subtitles

YouTube captions, OpenSubtitles search, and manual .srt / .vtt upload for language learning and accessibility.

04

Saved templates

Store named speed and volume combos — switch between Lectures and Movies without resetting every time.

05

Per-site memory

YouTube can stay at 1.5× while Udemy stays at 2×. Each domain remembers its own preference.

06

Chrome + Firefox

One product, two stores, one Pro license. Free covers everyday use; Pro unlocks the higher limits.
